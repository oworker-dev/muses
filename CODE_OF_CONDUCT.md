# Community Code of Conduct

Muses welcomes people who collaborate in good faith across design, media, product, research, engineering, and creative work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Respect privacy, consent, authorship, licenses, and cultural differences.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and meaningful use of generated material when relevant.
- Accept that product hypotheses, including a maintainer's own, must remain open to evidence.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, impersonation, unauthorized voice or likeness cloning, plagiarism, and publication of private or restricted material are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect contributors, users, the project, or third parties. Reports should be made privately to the repository owner. Enforcement decisions should disclose as much rationale as safety and privacy permit.
